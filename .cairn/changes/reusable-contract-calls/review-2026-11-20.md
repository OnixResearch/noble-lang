### M4 detail: restrictions on `chainClaim`

1. **Calls only.** `Chain.code` (L576–579) emits only calls, so a body with literals or builtins cannot be justified, e.g. S04's `[ swap d.sub2 ]`.
2. **Whole-stack steps.** Each step consumes the *entire* stack above the chain's single fixed `tail` (L617: `callFrame … tail args mid`). No caller value can sit below a callee's arguments, which Decision 3's frame discipline allows.
3. **Output-only post.** The conclusion is fixed to `pre := fun before _ _ => P before` and `post := fun _ after _ => Q after` (L630–631). The post cannot relate output to input. The relational claims of `twoStepClaim` (`out0 = in0 + 2`) and Decision 7 (`y = x+12`, L304) therefore need ghost-quantification glue that NamedV3 does not provide. Appendix A `twice_relational` derives `twoStepClaim`'s exact claim from `chainClaim` only with about ten lines of per-instance glue plus `BitVec.add_assoc`. "n-ary generalization of `twoStepClaim`" (L624–625, tasks L27) is not established by a theorem.

### M5 detail: consequences of identity placement

- **Injectivity is per step.** A chain takes an independent `EntryEmbedding` per step, so two distinct occurrences can share one slot. Appendix A `occurrences_alias` shows occurrences 1 and 2 both at slot 10 via `(·+9)` and `(·+8)`.
- **Two docstrings are contradicted.** L94–95 ("two distinct occurrences can never alias one row") and L128 ("Distinct occurrences also get distinct slots") do not hold for a chain.
- **Design requirements are unmodelled.** Decision 7's "fresh slot C ≠ A" (L293) and S05(c) "slot-reuse refusal" have no Lean counterpart.
- **Slot collisions across callees.** Two callee tables that use the same slot `k` for different rows cannot both be embedded in one caller (Appendix A `extends_collision`). Composing non-leaf callees from separately accepted modules therefore silently requires globally unique slot numbers, an unstated C1/envelope obligation.

## Answers

### 1. Do T2–T5 state what design.md Decision 3 requires?

- **T2 `callFrame` (NamedV3 L510–540): yes, for frame discipline.**

  - Typing is in the callee env (`subject.env`), which is equivalent for the I64/Bool interfaces.
  - Extra premises:
    - `bodies` and `profile` are the disclosed bounded profile. They are needed because the backward transport is false without them; see "The documented falsity" below.
    - `resolved` is redundant (m4).
  - No vacuity: Appendix A instantiates T2 through `chainClaim` with the real `NamedV2.capturedIncrementClaim`.
- **T3 `embed` (L493–503), `evaluate₂_env_free`/`holds₂_env_free` (L226–355): partly.**
