## Progress notes (not acceptance)

- T1 and T6 are landed in a separate `NamedV3` module (`proofs/mc1/NobleContracts/NamedV3.lean`): T1 `exec_append_iff` carries its own `run_append_iff` factoring lemma, and T6 is `holds_true₂` plus `holds_eq₂_iff` (equality holds exactly when both operands evaluate to the same named value — the named `.eq` compares values directly, not their `toInt`). Revisions 1 and 2 are untouched; `NamedV2.lean` is not edited (Decision 10).
- T2-T5 are landed in the same module, all pinned by `ContractGate.lean` under the strict axiom list (113 required theorems). **T3 `embed`** now has real vocabulary rather than prose: `Resolves` (every call occurrence of a row resolves in that row's table), `Extends` (the frozen import snapshot: the caller resolves every callee row at the same slot to the identical record), `Embedding` (the snapshot plus an injective occurrence→specialization-slot renaming with a freshness requirement) and `EntryEmbedding` (the caller's row carries the callee's accepted body, interface and provenance, with `input`/`output`/`origin` equalities). `run_forward` proves the snapshot simulates execution unconditionally; `run_backward` proves the converse on the bounded profile (`Sequential` bodies, `CallsResolve` static resolution) and documents why the general converse is false (a caller may add rows a callee leaves empty, and a stack program the callee cannot resolve can run only in the caller table). T3's second half is `evaluate₂_env_free`/`holds₂_env_free`: `Holds₂` is environment-independent for `maps`-free terms, and `maps` is the only constructor that consults the table.
- **T2 `callFrame`** uses the caller's own `tail` and the callee's `NamedV2.exportedNamedClaim` directly: for a resolved occurrence, typed arguments and the caller's single-call execution, it yields the frame-preserving result, the callee-env result typing and the callee's post. No callee body is re-derived and no second claim form is introduced.
- **T4 `liftMc1Claim`** lands with its reflection half: `run_reflect`/`exec_reflect` prove the named model adds no behavior *inside the `ProfileCode` fragment* (scalar literals and words 0, 1, 2, 4–7): a named return of a lifted profile program from a lifted stack is the lift of an MC1 return, `hasType_lift`/`codeTyped_lift`/`opTyped_lift`/`stackTyped_lift` lift MC1 typing, and `liftMc1Claim` states the MC1 claim's content at lifted tails with the named `StackTyped` boundary. The statement is deliberately tail-typed: the tail of `exportedNamedClaim` is an arbitrary named stack, and a named stack holding call-bearing programs has no MC1 preimage, so the MC1 claim can only be instantiated at the tails it quantifies.
- **T5 `chainClaim`** generalizes `twoStepClaim` to an arbitrary chain: `ChainStep` carries one resolved occurrence, its claim and the caller's pure `bridge`/`join` (propositions, never a program-call slice), `Chain` is the step list with the empty chain as identity, and `chainExec` transports the caller's assertion through the sequence with `T1`'s `exec_append_iff` plus `callFrame`; `chainClaim` then yields `exportedNamedClaim` from `root = Chain.code` plus the final typing.
