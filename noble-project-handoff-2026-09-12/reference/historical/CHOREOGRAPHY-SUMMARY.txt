## Important choices captured

**Choreography remains outside the kernel.** It adds an optional protocol library, checking/projection facility, and communication profile—not another expression form or extra parameters on every `Program<S,T,e>`. Ordinary `quote`, `compose`, `call`, and `reify` remain unchanged, preserving the existing small-core boundary. fileciteturn2file4L88-L100

**The first profile is deliberately finite and explicit.** It supports fixed logical roles, immutable-data transfers, local programs, participation scopes, and boolean choices. One role selects each branch, and every other member of that choice’s scope receives a notification. Protocol recursion, dynamic membership, replicated control evaluation, notification-elision inference, and in-session protocol switching are deferred.

**Ordering and failure have explicit meanings.** Choreographic sequence preserves each role’s order and communication dependencies; it is not a global barrier. A successful transfer means the receiver accepted the payload—not that subsequent remote work completed. Timeouts and cancellation do not imply an agreed branch, rollback, safe retry, or exactly-once business effects.

Live resources remain invocation inputs, never captured protocol state. The initial wire-data profile also excludes `Program`, `Syntax`, and resource-bearing values; portable code transport remains a separate loading and authorization problem.

## Examples and validation

The package includes **two structured examples**: request/response, and a worker/store protocol covering both success and failure. It also contains **68 conformance scenarios**, **16 proof obligations**, and **13 recorded implementation/transport boundaries**.

The [package checks passed](sandbox:/mnt/data/noble-choreography-0.1.0-draft.1/validation/report.json): all five patches applied exactly to their recorded baselines, requirement references and package-owned links resolved, and both examples passed fixture-metadata lint.

**No Noble execution or formal proof is claimed.** The runtime scenarios remain `not-run`, and the proof obligations remain `open`. These are generated draft specifications and updated working copies; the original Project sources were not overwritten.
