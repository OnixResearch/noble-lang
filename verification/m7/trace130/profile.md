# M7 bounded trace correspondence profile (issue #130)

Frozen before the first generated comparison. This lane is **finite execution evidence**, not a universal refinement proof or a new M7 theorem.

## Semantic relation and boundaries

Reference: [Synit Manual, Syndicated Actor Model, Dataspaces and Turns](https://synit.org/book/syndicated-actor-model.html). Applicable laws: assertions are owned and disappear on normal/exceptional owner termination; overlapping owners make one visible assertion until the final owner withdraws; a late subscription sees an already visible assertion; reassertion and repeated interest are idempotent; withdrawal propagates to surviving observers. Observation relates each admitted call's return/refusal class, `(live facets, assertions, interests)`, both exact `(name, ready)` membership bits, physical remaining facet slots and the ordered queue of `(observer identity, name, ready, change)` events. The queue is history, not current membership; explicit drain clears only the queue. Refusal leaves this entire observable state unchanged. A returned `false` for observing `(name,false)` means absence of **that exact pair**, not a false-valued assertion.

Noble deliberately narrows SAM: host-issued finite facet identities and parent/child retirement stand in for local ownership; at most one `(facet,name)` assertion with atomic readiness replacement; exact `(name,ready)` interests rather than general patterns; synchronous serialized host calls, with queued events instead of asynchronously delivered turns; finite physical slots that are not reused and explicit event capacity including all owed future removals. No entity references in values, general message transport, embedded capability references, cross-actor scheduler, distributed consistency or fairness. An individual synchronous publish commits before a subsequent compiled export traps: SAM's aborted-turn rollback **does not** roll back that earlier call. New traces do not prove full-SAM equivalence.
