noble-recurrent-external-author/v1
Frozen before an externally authored weight artifact is built or measured. This is a non-gating test of the checker interface, not an LLM experiment or a new optimized target.
The checker takes a source transition name, explicit exact-bit codec and candidate ONNX bytes. It must not read an emitter output or call the emitter, and must return typed ACCEPT or REFUSE for every well-formed candidate (no silent pass or traceback). Its named property is one-step simulation of the stack-swap transition (a,b)->(b,a) under binary32-exact/u64-bit-le/v1 over the 4x4 domain a,b in 0..3. A manually written ONNX Scan body with an independently specified 128-slot routing-index initializer is the candidate; the correct artifact must pass 16/16, a one-index faulty artifact must refuse and report the first (state,input) counterexample and pass count. Bind full candidate SHA256, parameter count and nonzero initializer count, plus corruption/configuration refusals. No behavioural accuracy or training claim. LLM-authored weights remain a separate non-gating arm and require a recorded full prompt, transcript, model identity, preregistered property/domain and independent submitted artifact before any verdict can be claimed.
