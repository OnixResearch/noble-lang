## 2. Verification of the Seven Guest Budget Categories

Verified against [`crates/noble-cli/src/calculator.rs`](../../../crates/noble-cli/src/calculator.rs) (`INPUT_LIMIT = 65_536`, `DEFAULT_BUDGET = [1_024, 256, 4_096, 32, 16, 4_000_000, 4_096]`, `BUDGET_NAMES`), [`.cairn/changes/calculator-v1/design.md`](../../../.cairn/changes/calculator-v1/design.md), and the guest `.noble` modules (`calculator-numeric.noble`, `calculator-expression.noble`, `calculator-display.noble`):

1. **`InputBytes` (slot 0, brackets `7` / `8` / `9`):** `expr.preparse_input_*` and `input_charge_*` debit the framed line including `\n` (`let x=7\n` = 8 B, `x+100000\n` = 9 B). At `7`, line 1 refuses `InputBytes [0,8)`, line 2 refuses `[0,9)`, `x` reports `UnknownName [0,1)`. At `8`, `let x=7` commits `7`, line 2 refuses, `x` prints `7`. At `9`, all three lines pass (`3/3` matched).
2. **`NumericDigits` (slot 1, brackets `2` / `3`):** `num@1.parse_digit_*` debits decimal digits excluding `.`. `123` has 3 digits: refuses `NumericDigits [2,5)` at `2`, commits `130` at `3`, prior `x=7` survives (`2/2` matched).
3. **`IntermediateIntegerBits` (slot 2, brackets `25` / `26`):** `parse_reserve_bounds` / `parse_reserve_bits` / `admit_bits` check `w = floor((3322 * d + 999) / 1000) + 2`. For `1234567` (`d = 7`), `w = floor(24253 / 1000) + 2 = 26`. At `25`, refuses `IntermediateIntegerBits [0,7)`; at `26`, outputs `1234567` (`2/2` matched).
4. **`SyntaxDepth` (slot 3, brackets `2` / `3`):** `parse_open_*` admits `depth + 1 <= budget[3]`. `(((x)))` reaches depth 3 at `[2,3)`, refuses at `2`, outputs `7` at `3` (`2/2` matched).
5. **`CallDepth` (slot 4, brackets `1` / `2`):** `call_enter` / `call_leave` charge active calls; `fn` definitions are silent and do not charge call depth. `f(1)` calling `g(1)` reaches depth 2: refuses `CallDepth [8,9)` at `1`, outputs `3` at `2`; follow-up `g(1)` receives a fresh budget and outputs `2` in both (`2/2` matched).
6. **`EvaluationWork` (slot 5, brackets `38051` / `38052` / `38053`):** Fresh budget per line. For `77\n`: preparse (`3`) + literal (`d=2 -> w=9 -> 50 * (2*9+1)^2 = 18_050`) + normalization (`width(77)=7, width(1)=1 -> g=10 -> (50+25+25) * 10^2 = 10_000`) + display (`100 * 10^2 = 10_000`) = **`38,053`**. `38051` and `38052` refuse `EvaluationWork [3,3)` and keep `x=7`; `38053` outputs `77` (`3/3` matched).
7. **`OutputSize` (slot 6, brackets `6` / `7`):** `charge_output_size` runs before binding commit in `Session::transact` (`let x=1234567` formats to 7 bytes). At `6`, both `let x=1234567` and `let y=1234567` refuse `OutputSize [14,14)` before commit, keeping `x=7` and `y` unbound (`UnknownName [0,1)`); at `7`, all commit (`2/2` matched).

## 4. Errata & Non-Blocking Caveats

1. **`plan.json` `revision.reason` v1-expectation erratum:** `plan.json` `revision.reason` states *"two expected silent recovery"*. Inspection of the pre-v2 plan (`540892a2^`) confirms v1 `control/worker-killed` expected `["7"]` with no `expect_status` (asserting line 2 would fail after `7`) and v1 `control/memory-cap` expected `[]` (silent `SIGTERM` kill). [`plan.json`](plan.json) is intentionally left byte-unchanged to preserve its `3f2d1bf3…` receipt binding, and the correction is recorded in [`README.md`](README.md) and [`.cairn/changes/calculator-v1/tasks.md`](../../../.cairn/changes/calculator-v1/tasks.md).
2. **External raw receipts:** Raw receipt `b1d66e0d31aa1821cc4c5266c345aa8acc54d154a6188d5a105effe355762034` (`cb5e9f71`), determinism rerun receipts `057e1c98…` and `bfc25c2b…` (`c5f6d8ea`), and per-session raw `stdin`/`stdout`/`stderr`/`events.json` remain external to the repository and were not re-hashed from git.
3. **`control/worker-killed` category label:** [`plan.json`](plan.json) classifies `control/worker-killed` under `"category": "native-failure"` while the observed failure is the fatal host restore refusal (`calculator restore failed: invalid bounded engine-worker protocol`, exit 2). Distinct `NativeFailure:` formatting and native step-quota separation (`runtime-exhausted`, never `ResourceLimit`) are covered separately by `calculator::tests::line_outcomes_map_to_the_public_streams_and_exit_status` and `native_step_quota_is_not_a_guest_resource_limit`, consistent with `plan.json` `non_claims[3]`.
4. **No `CALC-08` case promotion here:** Canonical `CALC-08` case promotion in [`specs/conformance/calculator-cases.json`](../../../specs/conformance/calculator-cases.json) stays separate under `CALC-78` ([`.cairn/changes/calculator-v1/tasks.md`](../../../.cairn/changes/calculator-v1/tasks.md)).
